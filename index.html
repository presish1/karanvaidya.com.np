Identity & Story

Karan Vaidya is a Nepali business leader and diplomat known for leadership in both corporate and international engagement.

He serves as Vice‑President of the Vaidya Group, a diversified family business involved in real estate, construction, pharmaceuticals, education, trading, automobiles and hospitality.

Business 360 Magazine
Education

Education &
Global Learning

Karan completed his undergraduate degree in Industrial Engineering at Purdue University. He then earned a dual International MBA from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology and Tsinghua University, gaining both Western and Eastern management perspectives.

During this program, he attended an exchange segment at London Business School as part of entrepreneurship and finance studies.

Purdue University · MIT · Tsinghua University · London Business School
